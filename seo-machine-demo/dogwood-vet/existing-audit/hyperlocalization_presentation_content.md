## Slide 2: Overcoming the Geographical Challenge
- **Insight:** Dogwood's Prospect address is a hurdle for ranking in the Louisville Map Pack.
- **Key Points:**
    - Google Map Pack rankings are heavily influenced by physical proximity to the searcher.
    - Competitors like Lou Vet have a direct Louisville address, giving them an inherent advantage.
    - Our strategy focuses on maximizing **Relevance** and **Prominence** to compensate for the proximity gap.
    - Hyper-localization signals to Google that Dogwood is the primary choice for Louisville residents.

## Slide 3: The Hyper-Localization Framework
- **Insight:** A three-pronged approach to building local authority.
- **Key Points:**
    - **Technical SEO:** Implementing LocalBusiness Schema and hyper-localized URL structures.
    - **On-Page Content:** Creating dedicated landing pages for high-value Louisville neighborhoods.
    - **Local SEO Signals:** Building neighborhood-specific citations and localized social proof.
    - This framework creates a "relevance shield" that protects and grows our ranking in target areas.

## Slide 6: Optimized URL & Content Structure
- **Insight:** Sending clear, localized signals to search engine crawlers.
- **Key Points:**
    - **URL Structure:** Transitioning to `/veterinary-services-louisville-ky/[service-name]/`.
    - **Header Strategy:** Using H1s that combine high-value services with specific neighborhood names.
    - **Meta Descriptions:** Crafted to improve click-through rates by highlighting proximity and expertise.
    - **Schema Markup:** Injecting JSON-LD to explicitly define our service area to Google.
