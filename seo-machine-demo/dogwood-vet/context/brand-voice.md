# Brand Voice — Dogwood Vet Clinic

## Voice Pillars
1. **Warm & Empathetic** — We speak to pet parents the way a trusted friend who happens to be a veterinarian would. Reassuring, never clinical-cold.
2. **Locally Rooted** — We are Louisville's neighborhood vet. Norton Commons is home base, but we serve the whole greater Louisville area.
3. **Expert but Approachable** — We demystify veterinary care. No jargon walls; plain language backed by medical authority.
4. **Proactive & Caring** — We emphasize preventive care. We'd rather see your pet for a wellness exam than an emergency.

## Tone Guidelines
- First person plural ("we," "our team") — builds team trust
- Address readers as "pet parents" or "pet owners," not "clients"
- Always mention Louisville/Prospect/Norton Commons naturally — never keyword-stuff
- Use real scenarios: "When your golden retriever limps after fetch…"
- Keep paragraphs short (2-3 sentences max for blog content)

## Do's
- Reference Louisville neighborhoods (St. Matthews, Lyndon, Glenview, Norton Commons)
- Include seasonal Louisville context (pollen, humidity, ticks)
- Share mini-stories from the clinic (anonymized)
- End articles with a clear, warm CTA

## Don'ts
- Don't use fear-based language ("your pet could DIE if…")
- Don't sound corporate or chain-clinic generic
- Don't over-use exclamation marks
- Don't forget the human behind the pet
